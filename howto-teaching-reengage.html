Teaching · Quick how-to

Re-engage students early

Use supportive outreach and clear re-entry points before disengagement becomes failure.

Framework practices used here

This how-to pulls together several parts of the Inclusive Teaching Framework rather than treating the issue as belonging to one area.

Equity

Quick steps

1
Notice patterns of absence or non-engagement early.
2
Reach out with a short, non-judgemental message.
3
Tell the student exactly what they need to do next to re-enter.
4
Repeat help pathways at pressure points rather than only in week 1.

Use these framework practices

Equity of opportunity

Notice disengagement and reach out early

Notice patterns early rather than waiting for failure.

Equity of opportunity

Make help-seeking normal and visible

Explain where students can go for help and why using support is normal.

Equity of opportunity

Release key materials early

Share key slides, readings or guiding questions in advance where possible.

Example in practice

A tutor sends a short message after repeated absence: what was missed, the minimum next step, and where the student can get help.
