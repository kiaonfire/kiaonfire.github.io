The Inclusive Teaching Hub helps UQ staff reduce avoidable barriers and make inclusive practice part of everyday curriculum, teaching and assessment. Inclusive teaching helps more students participate, understand what is expected and demonstrate what they know without unnecessary barriers.

Curriculum

Build inclusion into course and program design before students have to ask for it.

  • anticipate common barriers and varied student circumstances
  • make expectations, workload and hidden costs more visible
  • build accessibility and flexibility into the course from the start

Explore curriculum

Teaching

Create learning environments where participation feels possible and expectations are clear.

  • offer more than one legitimate way to participate
  • make disciplinary language and hidden norms visible
  • support students to follow, engage and ask for help

Explore teaching

Assessment

Measure the learning you intend to measure without adding avoidable barriers.

  • align assessment with the learning outcomes that matter
  • make instructions, criteria and feedback easier to understand
  • build flexibility and common adjustments into assessment design

Explore assessment
