Inclusive Teaching Hub · ITaLI

Inclusive teaching helps every student participate, belong and grow.

Our vision is learning where students can be here as they are, understand what is expected, encounter fewer avoidable barriers, and build the knowledge and confidence to succeed and contribute.

Why inclusive teaching matters →

Inclusive Teaching Framework

Turn the vision into practical changes.

The framework brings together four areas — Respect, Equity, Accessibility and Language — across curriculum, teaching and assessment. The four areas can be remembered as REAL.

Assessment
