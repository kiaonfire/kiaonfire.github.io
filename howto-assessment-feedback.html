Assessment

Back to Assessment

Why this matters

Give students feedback they can understand, access and use in their next piece of work.

What this looks like

Students can understand the feedback, access it in a usable form and see what to do next.

Steps to address it

1
Identify the most important next move rather than listing every weakness.
2
Use respectful language that treats the student as a developing learner.
3
Offer accessible feedback modes where feasible.
4
Create a later opportunity to apply the feedback.

Example in practice

A student receives the rubric, short written priorities and an optional audio explanation, then applies the feedback in the next assessment.
