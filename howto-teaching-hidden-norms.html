Back to Teaching

Why this matters

Help students learn how the discipline thinks and communicates instead of expecting them to infer it.

What this looks like

Key disciplinary terms, participation norms and tacit rules are explained rather than assumed.

Steps to address it

1
Name unfamiliar terms when they first appear.
2
Model how an expert approaches an unfamiliar problem.
3
Explain the purpose behind conventions, not only the rule.
4
Teach the digital tools and information practices students are expected to use.

Example in practice

Before asking students to “critically evaluate”, an educator models the process and names the questions an expert is asking at each step.
