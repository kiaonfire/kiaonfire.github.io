Teaching · Quick how-to

Explain disciplinary language and hidden norms

Help students learn how the discipline thinks and communicates instead of expecting them to infer it.

Framework practices used here

This how-to pulls together several parts of the Inclusive Teaching Framework rather than treating the issue as belonging to one area.

Language

Quick steps

1
Name unfamiliar terms when they first appear.
2
Model how an expert approaches an unfamiliar problem.
3
Explain the purpose behind conventions, not only the rule.
4
Teach the digital tools and information practices students are expected to use.

Use these framework practices

Language & literacies

Normalise unfamiliarity with academic language and norms

Invite students to ask about unfamiliar terms without embarrassment.

Language & literacies

Make disciplinary conventions visible

Show what the convention looks like and why it exists.

Teach the digital literacies students need

Identify which digital practices students are expected to use, from databases to citation tools.

Example in practice

Before asking students to “critically evaluate”, an educator models the process and names the questions an expert is asking at each step.
