A slightly meatier review

Review this area in five steps

  1. Choose one frequently used resource and check its heading structure, links, reading order and colour contrast.
  2. Check whether video, audio, diagrams and images provide equivalent access to essential information.
  3. Review how the material behaves at high zoom, on a narrow screen and with keyboard navigation.
  4. Identify any resource that requires an alternative format or inaccessible third-party tool.
  5. Record one immediate fix and one issue requiring wider platform, template or workflow change.

Barrier → response

Common barriers and practical responses

Image-only or scanned PDFs

Replace them with accessible source documents or a properly structured HTML version. Avoid relying on OCR alone.

Videos without accurate captions

Add and manually review captions. Use transcripts when they improve searching, review or access.

Complex tables

Simplify the table, identify headers and provide a text explanation for dense relationships.

Ultra content is hard to navigate

Use a consistent weekly structure, descriptive titles and one clearly named support area.

Before and after

Before

Lecture resources are uploaded as scanned PDFs and image-heavy slides.

  • No document structure
  • Text cannot reflow
  • Videos have automatic captions only

After

Accessible source files are published through a predictable Ultra structure.

  • Real headings and searchable text
  • Captions reviewed before publication
  • Equivalent descriptions for essential visuals
