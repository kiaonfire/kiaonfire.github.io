A slightly meatier review

Review this area in five steps

  1. List recurring extension, attendance, format, clarification and technology issues without exposing individuals.
  2. Check whether each underlying requirement is genuinely essential to learning, safety or accreditation.
  3. Identify which barriers can be removed universally and which still require individual support.
  4. Escalate patterns that cannot be resolved by one educator or one course.
  5. Report what changed, what remains unresolved and when the issue will be reconsidered.

Barrier → response

Common barriers and practical responses

Repeated extension requests

Review assessment clustering, notice periods, clarity, staging and reasonable baseline flexibility.

Repeated alternative-format requests

Improve accessible source documents and production workflows while retaining individual alternatives.

Repeated attendance adjustments

Review whether physical or synchronous presence is essential and provide equivalent access where it is not.

The same issue is passed between teams

Assign one coordinating owner and separate immediate support from systemic action.
