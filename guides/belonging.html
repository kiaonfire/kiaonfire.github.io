Strengthen belonging and professional identity

Do something now

Quick fixes

1

Review your examples

Check who appears as an expert, leader, innovator or decision-maker.

2

Name hidden expectations

Make disciplinary and professional norms explicit rather than rewarding insider knowledge.

3

Show varied pathways

Include non-linear careers, diverse settings and different ways of practising the profession.

4

Make repair possible

Explain how exclusionary behaviour will be addressed and how students can raise concerns.

A slightly meatier review

Review this area in five steps

  1. Map who is represented across readings, cases, images, guest speakers and professional examples.
  2. Identify where communities appear mainly as problems, patients, clients or recipients rather than knowledge holders.
  3. Separate genuine professional capabilities from inherited expectations about accent, personality, dress, gender or class.
  4. Review whether students can participate without hiding important parts of themselves or disclosing private experiences.
  5. Create one feedback route and one visible action showing that belonging concerns can influence the course.

Barrier → response

Common barriers and practical responses

Only one professional identity is visible

Use varied practitioners, pathways, communication styles and professional settings across core content.

Cultural knowledge is supplementary

Embed it as substantive intellectual content rather than an optional week or sidebar.

Professionalism is vaguely defined

Define it through ethics, responsibility, care, judgement and accountability.

Students fear raising exclusion

Provide confidential and collective feedback routes, explain how concerns are handled and report action taken.

Example redesign

Before and after

Before

Examples present a narrow professional profile and treat cultural difference as an occasional special topic.

After

Varied people, communities and pathways are visible throughout the course, and professional expectations are explicit and open to review.
