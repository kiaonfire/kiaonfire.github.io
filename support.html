Work with ITaLI

Bring a complex challenge and work through it with us.

Use ITaLI support when you are working on program-wide change, a difficult inclusion challenge, or a redesign that reaches beyond one page or one task.

Good moments to ask for help

  • Program or school-wide redesign
  • Complex assessment change
  • Accessibility and platform issues
  • Embedding the Inclusive Teaching Framework across multiple courses

Ways ITaLI can help

  • Review a course or program using the Inclusive Teaching Framework
  • Workshop inclusive teaching or assessment design
  • Translate student feedback into redesign priorities
  • Support prototype development and exemplars

Suggested intake prompt

What are you trying to change?

Describe the part of curriculum, teaching or assessment you want to improve, the barriers you are seeing, and which framework areas seem most relevant.
