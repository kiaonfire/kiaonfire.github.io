Quick steps

1
List the literacies students need to succeed in the course.
2
Identify which are explicitly taught and which are only assumed.
3
Add examples, glossaries, worked processes or short explanations at the point of need.
4
Sequence literacy development across the program rather than repeating or outsourcing it.
