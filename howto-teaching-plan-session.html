Teaching · Quick how-to

Plan an inclusive class or learning session

Use a quick planning routine to make live teaching clearer, safer and easier to participate in.

Framework practices used here

This how-to pulls together several parts of the Inclusive Teaching Framework rather than treating the issue as belonging to one area.

RespectAccessibility

Quick steps

1
State what students should be able to do by the end of the session.
2
Plan more than one way for students to engage with key ideas.
3
Build thinking and processing time into the session.
4
Make participation expectations clear and avoid unnecessary public risk.

Use these framework practices

Respect & representation

Set clear norms for respectful participation

Co-create or clearly state discussion norms early.

Accessibility

Build processing time into teaching

Pause after complex explanations and allow time to think.

Accessibility

Use multimodal teaching routinely

Present essential information in more than one way where appropriate.

Accessibility

Offer different ways to participate

Offer more than one way to contribute, such as speaking, posting or sharing through smaller groups.

Example in practice

A tutorial alternates short explanations with individual thinking time, small-group work and optional whole-room contribution.
