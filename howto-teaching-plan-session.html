Why this matters

Use a quick planning routine to make live teaching clearer, safer and easier to participate in.

What this looks like

The session has clear goals, a predictable structure, accessible materials and more than one way to engage.

Steps to address it

1
State what students should be able to do by the end of the session.
2
Plan more than one way for students to engage with key ideas.
3
Build thinking and processing time into the session.
4
Make participation expectations clear and avoid unnecessary public risk.

Example in practice

A tutorial alternates short explanations with individual thinking time, small-group work and optional whole-room contribution.
