Stories, videos and examples of practice

See how inclusive change happens in real courses.

This area is deliberately designed to grow. It can hold short videos, student stories, staff reflections, before-and-after examples, practice notes and longer case studies as new material becomes available.

Content principles

  • Student and staff contributors retain meaningful editorial input.
  • Trauma or personal disclosure is never required.
  • Videos include captions and transcripts.
  • Each story identifies what changed and what remains difficult.

Suggested story structure

Keep every contribution useful and accountable.

  1. Describe the course, context and barrier.
  2. Explain whose experience identified the issue and how they were involved safely.
  3. Show what changed in teaching, design, assessment or policy.
  4. Identify the result, limitations and anything that remains unresolved.
  5. Provide a reusable resource, prompt, template or next step.
