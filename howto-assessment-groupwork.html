Assessment · Quick how-to

Design more equitable group work

Structure collaboration so availability, confidence and social familiarity do not decide who succeeds.

Framework practices used here

This how-to pulls together several parts of the Inclusive Teaching Framework rather than treating the issue as belonging to one area.

EquityRespectLanguage

Quick steps

1
Make roles, scope and communication expectations explicit.
2
Use bounded collaboration windows rather than expecting constant availability.
3
Build in a midpoint check and a private escalation route.
4
Assess the intended collaborative capability rather than popularity or social polish.

Use these framework practices

Equity of opportunity

Structure group work for equitable participation

Set clear roles and expectations rather than leaving everything to social negotiation.

Respect & representation

Check cultural assumptions and hidden norms

Check whether the task rewards one style of expression when that style is not the learning outcome.

Language & literacies

Write clear assessment instructions and criteria

Write instructions in plain, direct language without losing disciplinary precision.

Example in practice

A team project uses a short group agreement, role planning and midpoint reset to surface workload and communication issues before they become crises.
