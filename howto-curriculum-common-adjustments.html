Curriculum & program design · Quick how-to

Build common adjustments into course design

Turn frequently requested individual adjustments into ordinary course features where the learning outcomes allow.

Framework practices used here

This how-to pulls together several parts of the Inclusive Teaching Framework rather than treating the issue as belonging to one area.

AccessibilityEquity

Quick steps

1
Look at the adjustments students request most often.
2
Separate supports that can be available to everyone from genuinely individual needs.
3
Build common access routes into the default course design.
4
Make the remaining individual support pathway easy to find and use.

Use these framework practices

Accessibility

Build common accessibility adjustments in by default

Identify the supports many students may need and design them in from the start.

Accessibility

Check materials and learning environments for accessibility

Adopt accessible document and media practices as standard workflow.

Equity of opportunity

Build flexibility into course structure and timelines

Check the weekly load, not just the total workload.

Example in practice

A course makes recordings, transcripts, accessible notes and a clear re-entry pathway standard rather than waiting for students to request them.
