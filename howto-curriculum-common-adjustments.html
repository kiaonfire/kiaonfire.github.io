Build common adjustments into course design

Turn frequently requested individual adjustments into ordinary course features where the learning outcomes allow.

Quick how-to

Curriculum

Back to Curriculum

Why this matters

Turn frequently requested individual adjustments into ordinary course features where the learning outcomes allow.

What this looks like

Recurring supports are built into the course from the start so students do not need to repeatedly disclose or negotiate for them.

Steps to address it

1
Look at the adjustments students request most often.
2
Separate supports that can be available to everyone from genuinely individual needs.
3
Build common access routes into the default course design.
4
Make the remaining individual support pathway easy to find and use.

Example in practice

A course makes recordings, transcripts, accessible notes and a clear re-entry pathway standard rather than waiting for students to request them.
