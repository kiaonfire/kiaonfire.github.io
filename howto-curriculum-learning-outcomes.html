Check your learning outcomes

Make sure outcomes describe the capabilities students actually need and do not hide unnecessary cultural or disciplinary assumptions.

Why this matters

Make sure outcomes describe the capabilities students actually need and do not hide unnecessary cultural or disciplinary assumptions.

What this looks like

Learning outcomes describe the capabilities students need without adding unnecessary assumptions about how those capabilities must be expressed.

Steps to address it

1
Underline the capability each outcome is really asking students to develop.
2
Check whether any wording assumes unexplained professional or disciplinary norms.
3
Ask whether inclusive or intercultural capability is genuinely part of good practice in the discipline.
4
Check that assessment can measure the outcome without requiring irrelevant modes or conventions.

Example in practice

A program rewrites a vague “professional communication” outcome to specify audience awareness, evidence use and clear explanation, making the capability easier to teach and assess.
