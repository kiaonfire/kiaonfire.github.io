Inclusive teaching areas

Explore the four areas of the Inclusive Teaching Framework in relation to course and program design. Use the links below to focus on one question or scan all four areas as part of a broader course review.

Respect & representation

Check whose identities, knowledge, norms and ways of participating are treated as legitimate.

  • Whose knowledge and perspectives are represented?
  • Are marginalised perspectives embedded meaningfully?
  • Do learning outcomes include inclusive capabilities where relevant?

Equity of opportunity

Check whether students with different lives have a realistic opportunity to participate and succeed.

  • Do I design for common barriers?
  • Is there flexibility in course structure and timelines?
  • Have I reduced hidden course costs?

Accessibility

Check whether materials, environments and activities are usable without unnecessary friction.

  • Are my learning materials and environments accessible?
  • Is it clear what content is essential?
  • Have I built common accessibility adjustments in?

Language & literacies

Make academic and disciplinary language, rules and expectations visible and learnable.

  • Do I teach the literacies students need?
  • Are terminology and expectations explicit?
  • Is language support built into the course?

Where to start

Common starting points

Start with the issue you are seeing rather than trying to work through the whole framework at once.

  • I’m seeing a lot of SAPs or adjustment requests come in
  • My course resources are too expensive
  • Students do not seem to know what is expected of them
  • I want to check whose knowledge and perspectives are represented
