Why this matters

Reduce avoidable cognitive and sensory load while keeping the disciplinary complexity that matters.

What this looks like

Students can follow the structure of a class, process key information in more than one way and revisit important content where needed.

Steps to address it

1
Chunk complex explanations and clearly signpost transitions.
2
Describe essential visuals and provide accessible source materials.
3
Pause after dense ideas so students can process before moving on.
4
Release key materials early enough for students who need preparation time.

Example in practice

A lecturer explains a complex diagram verbally, provides an accessible digital version and pauses between stages of the walkthrough.
