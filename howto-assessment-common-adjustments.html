Build common adjustments into assessment

Anticipate common disruptions and access needs so students do not have to repeatedly prove that their circumstances are legitimate.

Assessment

Back to Assessment

Why this matters

Anticipate common disruptions and access needs so students do not have to repeatedly prove that their circumstances are legitimate.

What this looks like

Common needs are anticipated in the task design instead of being handled only after individual students ask.

Steps to address it

1
Identify the extensions, alternatives or access adjustments students request most often.
2
Check which can be built into the task without changing the learning outcome.
3
Publish flexibility and recovery options before students need them.
4
Keep individual adjustment pathways for needs that cannot be met universally.

Example in practice

A course combines a simple short-extension pathway, an early checkpoint and equivalent format options so common disruptions do not require extensive disclosure.
